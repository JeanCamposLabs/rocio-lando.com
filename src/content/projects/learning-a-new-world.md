I partnered with **Jean Campos**, a web developer and content creator, to document his journey through new technology. Using a handmade, slightly cartoonish illustration style, we turned dense technical lessons into a story anyone could feel — the small victories, the 2 a.m. frustrations, the slow click of understanding.
