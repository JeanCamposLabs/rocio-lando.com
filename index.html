Rocío Landó is a professional illustrator and graphic artist who creates detailed ink and watercolor illustrations of architecture, animals, and nature, available for custom commissions and creative collaborations.

Rocío specializes in fine-line ink and watercolor work — from European and Caribbean architecture and houses to animals and botanical studies. Her style blends precise draftsmanship with a handmade, storytelling feel.

Most custom pieces take 1–2 weeks, depending on size and level of detail. Larger or highly intricate works can take longer, and timelines are always confirmed before a project begins.

Pricing depends on the size, detail, and intended use of the artwork. After a short brief you'll receive a tailored quote — email contact@rocio-lando.com to get started.
